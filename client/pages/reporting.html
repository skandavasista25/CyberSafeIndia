Cyber Crime Reporting

Speed matters. Reporting within the first hour gives banks the best chance of freezing stolen funds before they're withdrawn.

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal

The Government of India's cybercrime.gov.in portal lets anyone file a complaint about financial fraud, online harassment, or other cyber crimes, 24 hours a day. Complaints related to financial fraud are prioritised and routed toward fund recovery. You can track your complaint status using the acknowledgement number provided after filing.

Step by step

How to report cyber fraud

Call the 1930 helpline immediately

This is the fastest route to alert your bank and initiate a fund freeze request before money moves further.

File a complaint on cybercrime.gov.in

Create an account, select the relevant category (e.g. financial fraud), and fill in transaction details, screenshots, and any messages received.

Note down your acknowledgement number

This number lets you track your complaint's progress and is required if you need to follow up with your bank or local police.

Visit your bank branch if needed

For larger amounts, follow up in person with your bank's fraud department, carrying your complaint acknowledgement and transaction details.

File an FIR at your local police station if advised

For significant losses, the cyber cell or local police may ask you to file a formal First Information Report to support further investigation.

The first hour matters most

What to do immediately after becoming a victim

Call 1930 right away

Report the fraudulent transaction before the funds can be moved further by the scammer.

Block your card or freeze your account

Use your bank's app or helpline to immediately block the affected card or UPI account.

Change your passwords and PIN

Update your net banking password, UPI PIN, and email password from a different, secure device.

Save all evidence

Screenshot messages, calls, transaction IDs, and the scammer's UPI ID or phone number before reporting.

File your complaint on cybercrime.gov.in

Submit full details while the transaction is still fresh — this speeds up any potential fund recovery.

Watch for signs of SIM swap

If your phone suddenly loses network signal, contact your telecom provider immediately — it may indicate a SIM swap in progress.
