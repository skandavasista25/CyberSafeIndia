Everyday habits

Security Tips

None of these take more than a few minutes — together, they close most of the doors scammers rely on.

Keep software updated

Updates patch security flaws that attackers actively exploit. Turn on automatic updates for your phone's OS, apps, and computer.

Use antivirus software

A reputable antivirus or built-in security suite catches malicious files and links before they can do damage.

Enable device lock

A PIN, pattern, or biometric lock on your phone and laptop prevents anyone who picks it up from accessing your apps and messages.

Avoid public Wi-Fi for banking

Public networks at cafes or airports are easier to intercept. Use mobile data or a trusted personal hotspot for anything financial.

Verify websites before entering details

Check for HTTPS and a correctly spelled domain before typing in a password, card number, or personal information.

Download apps only from trusted sources

Stick to the official Play Store or App Store. Avoid installing apps from links sent over SMS, email, or chat.

Back up important data regularly

Keep copies of photos and documents on cloud storage or an external drive, so ransomware or device loss doesn't mean permanent loss.

Review app permissions

Periodically check which apps have access to your contacts, SMS, camera, and location, and revoke anything unnecessary.
